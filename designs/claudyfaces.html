WHAT CHANGED FROM V1
Eyes went from 40px to 96px and moved up to y=104–140, so the face owns the top two thirds of the panel. Brows are new and carry most of the expression: flat and low for idle, raised for attention, stair-stepped inward for danger. The always-on 7px ring is gone — only attention, ratelimit and danger draw on the bezel, which makes those three read as urgent instead of decorative. The state word drops to 20px Silkscreen with the detail line at 13px, both pushed to y=314+ where the circle narrows.
Porting is unchanged and slightly cheaper: fewer, larger rects per face, no rotated elements at all (the failed X is a 3×3 grid, the danger brow is three offset slabs), so nothing needs a transform in LVGL. Every y position above is a literal lv_obj_set_pos value on a 412 canvas.
